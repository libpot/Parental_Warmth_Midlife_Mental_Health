# Long-Term Associations of Parental Warmth with Mental Health: Cross-National Differences and Psychosocial Pathways Across the First Half of Life
Early caregiving experiences may shape mental health well beyond adolescence, yet evidence on such long-term associations across populations and their potential psychosocial pathways remains limited. Here, we applied a causal inference approach to data from population-based cohorts from the United Kingdom and Norway spanning multiple decades. Our main aim was to examine the relationship between parental warmth assessed in adolescence and mental health across three midlife outcome domains: psychological distress, psychosocial well-being, and substance use. Applying a causal mediation approach, we further investigated whether adult social relationships could account for potential protective relationships.

This repository contains complete analytical code, including data preprocessing, main analyses, and sensitivity analyses, separately for each examined cohort.
